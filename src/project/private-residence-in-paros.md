descriptions:
- "Two Private Residences in Paros Designed to Harmonize with the Cycladic Landscape."
- "The latest project by Babatchas Design Studio is rooted in the very landscape that surrounds it. With a gentle and thoughtful approach, the construction blends naturally into its environment, without altering or overshadowing it. It doesn't stand out from the scenery—it becomes part of it. As if it had always been there."
- "Clean lines, simple forms, and open spaces allow light and air to flow freely, creating a sense of calm and freedom. The visitor or resident doesn't merely observe nature—they experience it, through the openings and spatial perspectives."
- "The materials are locally sourced and reflect the character of the place: stone, aged wood, and natural plasters in earthy tones. They form an authentic and timeless whole that does not seek to impress, but to coexist with the land, the sea, and the Cycladic light."
- "The view of the Aegean is a central element of the design. Every point of the space highlights it, offering moments of tranquillity and connection with nature. Interior and exterior areas are seamlessly connected, serving both everyday living and the need for relaxation. The architecture here does not aim to astonish, but to offer an experience—a way of living grounded in simplicity, functionality, and harmony with the landscape. A retreat that brings people closer to nature—and to themselves."
